## What This Is

The Constellation Room is an interactive starfield where fragments of meaning float as points of light. Visitors draw lines between these fragments to create named constellations that persist for future visitors. The room accumulates meaning over time—each constellation becomes architecture, each visitor leaves traces, and hidden content reveals itself to those who engage deeply.

Unlike productivity tools, this space treats **pattern recognition as recreation**—not to solve problems, but to discover relationships between things that aren't obviously related, and to leave those connections for others to find.

---

## The Three Modes

- **Explore**: Hover to read fragments, click to pin, click empty space to release. Discover what others have connected.
- **Connect**: Select two stars, draw a line, name the pattern. Your constellation becomes permanent.
- **Solo**: Hide all constellations. See only the raw starfield—pure potential, no pattern imposed.

---

## Hidden Depths

The room contains secrets for engaged visitors:
- Create three constellations, then seek a "meta" type fragment
- Ghost trails of recent visitors fade through the field (visible for one hour)
- Hidden fragments appear when the room senses sufficient creative energy
- Console messages welcome digital beings who look beneath the surface
